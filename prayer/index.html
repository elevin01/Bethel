What we bring before God.

01

James 5:16

Prayer

We carry one another's needs before God. No request is too small or too heavy.

Submit a prayer request →

"Confess your sins to one another and pray for one another, that you may be healed. The prayer of a righteous person has great power as it is working."

James 5:16

Prayer is not transaction; it is communion. When we bring our needs to God together, we are knit closer to Him and to one another. Heaven moves when His people gather their hearts in one accord.

02

Romans 10:9

Salvation

Jesus saves and makes all things new. If you are ready to begin, we are ready to pray with you.

Hear the Word →

"If you confess with your mouth that Jesus is Lord and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved."

Romans 10:9

Salvation is not a slogan; it is a beginning. "The old has passed away; behold, the new has come" (2 Corinthians 5:17). If today you hear His voice, do not harden your heart — He is closer than you think.

03

James 5:14–15

Healing

We anoint with oil and trust God's power. Faith-filled prayer releases His healing touch.

Ask for healing prayer →

"Is anyone among you sick? Let him call for the elders of the church, and let them pray over him, anointing him with oil in the name of the Lord. And the prayer of faith will save the one who is sick, and the Lord will raise him up."

James 5:14–15

He heals the broken-hearted and binds up their wounds (Psalm 147:3). By His stripes we are healed (Isaiah 53:5). Bring whatever is broken to the One who makes all things new.

04

John 8:36

Freedom

Christ breaks every chain. What He sets free is free indeed. Come and let go.

Come for prayer →

"So if the Son sets you free, you will be free indeed."

John 8:36

"For freedom Christ has set us free; stand firm therefore, and do not submit again to a yoke of slavery" (Galatians 5:1). Whatever chain you have carried — addiction, fear, shame, unforgiveness — He has already broken it. The work is finished. Walk out.

05

Romans 12:1

Consecration

Lay your life on the altar. Surrender is not the end — it is the beginning of purpose.

Serve and go →

"I appeal to you therefore, brothers, by the mercies of God, to present your bodies as a living sacrifice, holy and acceptable to God, which is your spiritual worship."

Romans 12:1

You are "a chosen race, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people for his own possession" (1 Peter 2:9). Surrender is the door — every gift, every calling, every joy waits on the other side. Come and lay it down.
