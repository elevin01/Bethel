What to expect ↗ Get directions ↗
Which service should I come to?

Our English service starts at 9:30 AM and our Malayalam service starts at 10:30 AM. Choose the language you’re most comfortable with.

Can I bring my children?

Yes. Children are welcome to worship with their families. We don’t currently have a separate nursery or children’s program.

What should I wear?

Come in whatever you feel comfortable wearing. You don’t need to dress a certain way to join us.

Do I need to sign up?

No registration is needed to attend a Sunday service. Simply come along.
