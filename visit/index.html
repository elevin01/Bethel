What to know

Everything you need before you arrive.

What to Expect

Warm welcome, Spirit-led worship, Bible-centered message, and room to respond.

Service lasts about 90 minutes. Prayer is available after.

"I rejoiced when they said to me, 'Let us go to the house of the Lord.'" Psalm 122:1

Kids & Families

Kids worship with their families. We're a small congregation and we love having little ones in the room.

No separate kids' program or nursery yet — that's coming as we grow.

"Let the children come to me and do not hinder them." Mark 10:14

Where & Parking

We meet in the lower level of Golden Sword Church at 10 Tanglewylde Ave.

Street parking is plentiful; metered spots are down the block — meters are off on Sundays.
